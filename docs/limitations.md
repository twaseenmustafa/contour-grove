# What to know

Contour Grove is an early, local-first editor. It's not yet a full replacement for Figma. Please keep project backups, especially for important work.

- **Your work stays in the browser.** Browser storage can be cleared or unavailable; switching browsers or website addresses can make saved projects inaccessible. Export a project backup to keep an editable copy.
- **Tracing is an approximation.** It follows visible shapes and cannot recover original layers, fonts or hidden details. Complex textures and gradients may not survive.
- **SVG import is limited.** Text, gradients, embedded images and filters are among the unsupported features. Exported SVGs with live text or effects may not reopen in the editor; keep a project backup.
- **Scissors and groups have limits.** Scissors works on eligible ungrouped vectors; effects and some other cases are unsupported. Group resize and rotation, nested groups and editable SVG patterns are not yet supported.
- **Text depends on available fonts.** Text is single-line with a limited choice of fonts. Exported live text may look different on another device.
- **Size and performance are limited.** Projects, assets and shapes have size limits; very large or complex designs may be slow.
- **AI and MCP are not connected.** The local automation workbench accepts validated commands, but there is no live MCP connection or hosted AI service.
